A field guide to motion design

Sixteen ways to move a frame.

Every motion style starts as graphic design: a palette, a shape language, a type voice. Then it adds its own rules for timing and movement. Each style below is a seamless loop made in After Effects or Blender.

The styles

    How these were made

    After Effects (12 styles). Built from shape layers, expressions and effects, with every move timed to a 6-second cycle so the loop never visibly restarts.

    Blender (4 styles). Clay 3D, Y2K Chrome, Isometric and Low Poly were modelled, lit and rendered in Blender, then encoded for the web.

    The web. Videos load only when they scroll into view and pause when they leave. With reduced motion turned on, nothing autoplays.

    Motion Styles. Made with After Effects, Blender and plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
